I am a Ph.D. candidate in the **Construction Automation, Robotics, and Ergonomics (CARE) Lab** at Purdue University's Bowen School of Construction, advised by Dr. Yunfeng (Cindy) Chen, and expecting to graduate in May 2027.

My work combines computer vision, markerless motion capture, and wearable physiological sensing to measure ergonomic risk in field maintenance work. I run studies with highway maintenance crews at the Indiana Department of Transportation, testing how interventions such as passive back exoskeletons and ergonomic handles change the physical demands of the job.
